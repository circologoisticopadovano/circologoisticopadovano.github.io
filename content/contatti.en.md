Want to come and play, have questions, or simply want to know more? Write to us or
drop by during one of our meetings: beginners are always welcome.

## When we meet

We meet on the **second Wednesday of every month, from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM**.

If you would like to arrive before the meeting time, you can work remotely
(**smart working**) at the venue.

## Where we are

**Centro Parrocchiale San Carlo Borromeo**
Via Pietro Giovanni Guarneri, 22 — 35132 Padua (PD), Italy

[Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Centro+Parrocchiale+San+Carlo+Borromeo+Via+Pietro+Giovanni+Guarneri+22+Padova)

## How to reach us

The easiest way to reach us is the club's **Telegram group**: you'll find the
members, up-to-date meeting times, and you can introduce yourself with no
commitment — even just to satisfy a curiosity.

- 💬 [Join the club's Telegram group](https://t.me/+aqclA6LwvHtlNGQ0)

Or simply drop by one of our meetings: beginners are always welcome.

See you at the goban.
