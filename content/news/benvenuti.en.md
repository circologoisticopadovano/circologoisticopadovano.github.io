It is with great pleasure that we launch the **official website of the Circolo
Goistico Padovano**! 🎉

From today this will be our online point of reference for staying up to date on
everything about Go in Padua:

- 📰 **Club news and events**
- 🏆 **Tournaments** and meetings with other clubs
- 📚 **Resources** to learn and improve
- 📍 **Practical information** on schedules and venue

Keep following us: we will post all the news here. And if you have never played
Go, take a look at the [How to play Go](/en/how-to-play/) page and [come and
visit us](/en/contact/) at one of our meetings.

See you soon at the goban! ⚫⚪
