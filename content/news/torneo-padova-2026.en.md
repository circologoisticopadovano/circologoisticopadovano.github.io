The **Circolo Goistico Padovano** is delighted to announce its Go tournament! 🏆⚫⚪

A day dedicated to the game of Go, open to players of every level — from beginners
to the most experienced. We look forward to seeing many of you at the goban!

> ⚠️ **The details below are provisional** and will be confirmed shortly.

## Practical information

- **📅 Date:** Saturday 3 October 2026 *(to be confirmed)*
- **🕘 Schedule:** check-in and registration from 9:00 AM, first game at 10:00 AM *(to be confirmed)*
- **📍 Venue:** Centro Parrocchiale San Carlo Borromeo — Via Pietro Giovanni Guarneri, 22, 35132 Padua (PD), Italy
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Centro+Parrocchiale+San+Carlo+Borromeo+Via+Pietro+Giovanni+Guarneri+22+Padova)
- **💶 Entry fee:** to be defined *(to be confirmed)*
- **🍝 Lunch:** available on reservation *(details to be confirmed)*

For any questions you can contact us via the
[Telegram group](https://t.me/+aqclA6LwvHtlNGQ0) or at the contacts on the
[Contact](/en/contact/) page.

## Rules

> ⚠️ **Provisional draft** — to be refined before the final publication.

- **Playing system:** round robin with a Swiss system (McMahon).
- **Number of rounds:** 5 *(to be confirmed)*.
- **Thinking time:** to be defined (e.g. 45 minutes per player + byo-yomi) *(to be confirmed)*.
- **Komi:** 6.5 in even games.
- **Handicap:** assigned based on the rank difference *(to be confirmed)*.
- The rounds you plan to take part in are **indicative**: they become official if
  there are no other indications from the player 10 minutes before the games.
