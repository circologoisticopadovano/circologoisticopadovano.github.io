Welcome to the website of the **Circolo Goistico Padovano**, the meeting point for fans of
the **game of Go** in Padua and the surrounding area.

Go is an ancient game of strategy born in China over 2,500 years ago: dead-simple
rules, infinite depth. Whether you are an experienced player or have never placed
a stone on the goban, you are welcome here.

## What we do

- 🀄 **Free games** among members, at every level
- 📚 **Lessons and introductions** to the game for beginners
- 🏆 **Tournaments and meetings** with other clubs
- 🤝 Spreading and promoting Go in the area

Take a look at our [News & Events](/en/news/), find out [how to get
started](/en/how-to-play/) or [come and visit us](/en/contact/): your first game
is always on the club!
