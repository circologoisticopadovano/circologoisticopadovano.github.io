The **Circolo Goistico Padovano** brings together the people in Padua who share a passion for
**Go**, the oldest and most fascinating board game of strategy in the world.

## Our story

We are a group of enthusiasts who meet regularly to play, study and spread Go. The
club was born from the desire to create a stable point of reference for Go players
in the Padua area, and to offer the curious a welcoming place to take their first
steps.

## What we offer

- **A place to play** at every level, from the absolute beginner to the dan-level
  player.
- **Study support**: game reviews, problems (tsumego) and learning material.
- **Events and tournaments**, both internal and in collaboration with other clubs
  and with the Italian Go Federation (FIGG).
- **Promotion of Go** in schools, libraries and city events.

## Who we are for

Anyone: no experience or equipment needed. All it takes is curiosity and the
willingness to learn. Beginners are especially welcome — in fact, they are the
heart of what we do.
